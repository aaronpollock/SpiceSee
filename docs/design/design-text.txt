08 · App icon & .vv document icon
App icon — mark study, 176 / 96 / 48 / 32 / 16 pt
1024 px master
128
48
32
16
Concept: a display, seen head-on, with a single chili pepper as the thing on screen — "spice" you can see. Two shapes only, so it survives 16px.
Legibility ladder: 128 keeps the stand; 48 and below drop it; 16 drops the stem and the bezel highlight, leaving a dark rectangle + red form (the strongest 2-value silhouette).
Pepper red #D0392A at small sizes / gradient #E4523E→#A82419 at large. Stem #3E7A3A. Screen #191A1D. Plate is a neutral light-grey squircle — no colour behind the pepper, so it stays the only saturated element in the Dock.
Deliver as .icon (Icon Composer, macOS 26) plus a 1024 PNG set for 14/15; light, dark and tinted variants — dark plate = #2C2C2E→#1C1C1E, pepper unchanged.
